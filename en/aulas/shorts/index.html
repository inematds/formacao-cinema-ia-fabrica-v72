Lesson 5 · 18 min

Shorts with a quality gate: hook, 9:16 cut and QA

A short that loses its first three seconds doesn't get a second chance. MakeShorts puts two gates in the way: one on the script, which measures the hook before you record, and one on the video, which measures format, sound and frozen screen before you publish. In this lesson you fail and pass both, using dry-run files.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The three layers of the hook: phrase, scene, and text on frame zero
  2. 2The script gate: short hook, banned opening, keyword up front
  3. 3The video gate: 1080×1920, 30 fps, h264/aac, −14 LUFS, no black screen
  4. 4cardshorts: a narrated card short, five styles

Goal: write a hook that passes the script gate, and fail or approve a 9:16 video with the makeshorts QA.

02 · The hook in three layers

Write all three before you record, and check that each one holds up on its own.

1. Phrase

What you say in the first two seconds. Get straight to it, with minimal context: no introduction.

Example"Your video dies in the first three seconds."

2. Scene

What appears on frame zero. It has to stop the thumb of someone scrolling with the sound off: a contrast, a number, an object out of place.

3. Text

A very short summary highlighted on screen. Someone who can't hear the phrase decides by it.

4. Turn

After the hook, contradict a common belief of the audience. That's what holds them until the proof. The full 7-step reel is in the social media course.

03 · The script gate

Two scripts from the kit: one that passes and one that fails. The keyword is "gancho".

File · kit/exemplos/a5-roteiros/fraco.md
# Roteiro fraco
### FALA
Olá pessoal, hoje eu vou mostrar para vocês uma coisa muito interessante sobre como fazer vídeos curtos que prendem a atenção das pessoas. O gancho tem três camadas: a frase, a cena e o texto. Fica até o fim que no final eu conto o segredo.
Terminal · check the scripts
K=~/projetos/formacao-cinema-ia-fabrica-v72/kit
cd ${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-fabrica/a5; Q=makeshorts/.claude/skills/makeshorts/scripts
python3 $Q/qa_roteiro.py "$K/exemplos/a5-roteiros/bom.md" --keyword gancho > bom.txt; echo "bom: $?"
python3 $Q/qa_roteiro.py "$K/exemplos/a5-roteiros/fraco.md" --keyword gancho > fraco.txt; echo "fraco: $?"
grep -hE "❌|RESULTADO" bom.txt fraco.txt
Example from the repository
bom: 0
fraco: 1
RESULTADO: APROVADO (falta: teste da lacuna, tese, fontes — revisão humana)
  ❌ gancho (palavras)        23 palavras: “Olá pessoal, hoje eu vou mostrar para vocês uma coisa muito interessante sobre c” (máx. 9)
  ❌ abertura proibida        começa com “ola”
  ❌ palavra-chave no início  “gancho” fora das primeiras 15 palavras (≈ 5 s)

The APROVADO on the script flags what the machine doesn't measure: whether the hook opens a gap, whether the thesis takes a side, whether the facts have sources. That part is up to you.

04 · The video gate

The block clones makeshorts at a fixed commit, makes a 20 s dry-run video at 1080×1920 with normalized sound, and runs the QA on the promo profile.

Terminal · QA of a dry-run short
E=${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-fabrica/a5; rm -rf "$E"; mkdir -p "$E"; cd "$E"
git clone -q https://github.com/inematds/makeshorts.git && git -C makeshorts checkout -q 52eb799
Q=makeshorts/.claude/skills/makeshorts/scripts
ffmpeg -v error -f lavfi -i "testsrc2=s=1080x1920:r=30:d=20" -f lavfi -i "sine=f=220:d=20" \
  -af loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-2:LRA=7 -ar 48000 -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -shortest ensaio-916.mp4
python3 $Q/qa_short.py ensaio-916.mp4 --profile divulgacao > qa.txt; echo "saída: $?"
grep -E "^(✅|⚠️|❌)|RESULTADO" qa.txt
Terminal · the same QA on a 16:9 video
cd ${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-fabrica/a5; Q=makeshorts/.claude/skills/makeshorts/scripts
ffmpeg -v error -y -f lavfi -i "testsrc2=s=1920x1080:r=30:d=20" -f lavfi -i "sine=f=220:d=20" \
  -af loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-2:LRA=7 -ar 48000 -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -shortest ensaio-169.mp4
python3 $Q/qa_short.py ensaio-169.mp4 --profile divulgacao > qa169.txt; echo "saída: $?"
grep -E "^❌|RESULTADO" qa169.txt
Example from the repository
saída: 0
✅ duração                  20.0s
✅ formato                  1080x1920
✅ fps                      30.00
✅ codec vídeo              h264
✅ codec áudio              aac
✅ loudness                 -13.9 LUFS
✅ sem tela preta inicial   imagem desde o frame 0
✅ frame 0 (informação)     0.063 de bordas
⚠️  trechos parados          1 acima de 4 s: 0.0–20.0s — justificar ou quebrar
⚠️  legendas                 não verificadas (passe --srt ou --words); reel sem legenda conferida não vai pro Nei
RESULTADO: APROVADO (falta a revisão humana: gancho, prova, pronúncia)

The numbers (−13.9 LUFS, 0.063 of edges) are from this machine, ffmpeg 6.1.1; on yours they may vary a little. What matters is the RESULTADO line. The captions warning refers to Nei's flow: in yours, pass --srt.

saída: 1
❌ formato                  1920x1080
RESULTADO: REPROVADO

Only the format failed: the rest of the video is the same. A horizontal video doesn't become a short by renaming the file.

05 · The contract's three profiles

Duration in seconds and spoken words, from references/reel-profiles.json (contract 1.2.1).

1. divulgacao

Minimum 15 s, ideal 20 to 50 s, maximum 59 s. Speech of 60 to 125 words.

2. tutorial

Minimum 25 s, ideal 35 to 60 s, maximum 75 s. Speech of 90 to 150 words.

3. mini-aula

Minimum 45 s, ideal 60 to 110 s, maximum 119 s. Speech of 150 to 280 words.

06 · The second engine: cardshorts

roteiro.yaml becomes a narrated 9:16 card short. MIT license. To render, it needs a local voice and image (inemavox and an image server with a GPU); here you only list the styles.

Terminal · cardshorts styles
cd ${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/ensaio-fabrica/a5
git clone -q https://github.com/inematds/cardshorts.git && git -C cardshorts checkout -q d7f5c6d
cd cardshorts && python3 cardshorts.py estilos | grep -E "^[a-z]" 
grep -E "^(estilo|max_seg):" exemplos/versus/roteiro.yaml
Example from the repository
celular    celular — interface de telefone: notificação, busca, fila e enquete SIM/NÃO
cracha     cracha — alvo, crachás carimbados e cédula; fundo grafite, acento vermelho
documento  documento — papel datilografado, formulário em branco, carimbos e certificado
extrato    extrato — nota fiscal / extrato bancário com borda picotada e carimbos
versus     versus — editorial preto e branco com vermelho; foto x foto, telas divididas, chamadas em Anton
estilo: versus
max_seg: 30          # a velocidade da fala é ajustada para caber (até 1,2x)

The max_seg is a ceiling: speech is sped up to 1.2× to fit and, if it still doesn't fit, the engine fails instead of cutting.
